Main element

Its opposite shows as an outlined orb, its orbs seat first, and dice use its color. Pick None for common cards with no element or mana cost.

Name and cost

Type letters or tap the orbs: N Nature, A Arcana, R Radiant, V Violence, C Calmness, D Demonic.

How orbs are placed

Each letter is one orb around the art. An element's first orb sits in its own seat and more spread to either side, up to 5, never into its opposite's seat. Opposites: Radiant–Demonic, Calmness–Violence, Arcana–Nature.
